## 0.2.5

Initial public review candidate for the Hellotracks Zapier integration.

1. New trigger/new_job polls recently created Hellotracks jobs.
2. New trigger/updated_job polls recently updated Hellotracks jobs.
3. New trigger/completed_job polls completed Hellotracks jobs.
4. New create/create_job creates or upserts Hellotracks jobs.
5. New create/update_job updates Hellotracks jobs.
6. New create/archive_job archives Hellotracks jobs.
7. New create/delete_job deletes Hellotracks jobs.
8. New search/find_job finds Hellotracks jobs by External ID.
9. New search/find_member finds Hellotracks members by email, username, name, or UID.
